About PHCM

Our story, people and purpose


Who We Are

Perpetual Help College of Manila is a private college in Sampaloc, Manila, and part of the University of Perpetual Help System.

Mission

The institution is committed to shaping dynamic leaders who are physically, intellectually, socially, and spiritually developed to attain a high quality of life. As a provider of health and education services, it trains research-oriented Christian professionals dedicated to community care, quality education, and the core philosophy of helping others to build the nation.

Vision

The University of Perpetual Help System aspires to be a premier institution delivering innovative education and research to foster academic excellence, community partnerships, and industry linkages. It aims to develop globally competitive Filipinos rooted in strong moral values, envisioning a peaceful, prosperous society valued by future generations.
